Frequently asked

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is stored locally in your browser's localStorage. CreatorOS AI runs fully offline in this standalone build.

Are the numbers real?

No — all clients, invoices and earnings are sample demo data. Invoices are clearly marked as demo records and no payments are processed.

Do Connections actually link my apps?

Not yet. Connections are a frontend demonstration. A clean adapter structure is included in the code for future real integrations.

How do I download an invoice?

Open the invoice preview and choose Print — your browser's print dialog supports "Save as PDF".
