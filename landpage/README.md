# Levia AI — Flagship Application Landing Page
### Your creativity. Your business. One intelligent workspace.

A complete, high-fidelity SaaS landing page combining **Reference Image 1's structural blueprint** (layout, composition, abstract flowing wave geometry, floating spheres, left-aligned hero content, navigation, and carousel dots) with **Reference Image 2's visual identity** (dark cosmic background, purple lighting, glowing neon contours, dimensional sphere shading, and futuristic atmosphere).

---

## 🚀 12-Section Architecture & Feature Map

1. **01. Navigation**: Brand logo (Levia AI), links (`Home`, `Features`, `Workspaces`, `Beluga AI`, `About`), and primary CTA `Get Started →`.
2. **02. Hero Section**: Seamless full-bleed canvas (`100vw × 100vh`) with fluid wave SVG, ultra-translucent glass spheres (98% optical clarity, specular glints, and electric purple crescent rims), 4-dot interactive persona carousel, and floating slide-down scroll indicator.
3. **03. The Problem ("Less Chaos. More Creation.")**: Highlighting the 3 fundamental friction points creators face: *Too many tools*, *Too much repetitive work*, and *Too little context*.
4. **04. Personalized AI Workspace ("Built Around You")**: Dedicated double-bezel cards for the 4 core creator personas:
   - **Content Creator**: Content Studio, scripts, captions, and publishing calendar.
   - **Product Creator**: Beluga AI integration, product intelligence, and lead discovery.
   - **Freelancer & Template Seller**: Client management, project progress, deliverables, and invoices.
   - **Educator**: Educational courses, learning resources, and teaching productivity.
5. **05. The Personalization Engine**: Context-driven assistance through *Your Creative Context*, *More Relevant Suggestions*, and *A Workspace That Evolves*.
6. **06. Content Studio**: Interactive ideation and drafting studio with AI suggestion callouts and scheduled publishing queue.
7. **07. Beluga AI (Product Intelligence & Lead Discovery)**: Explainable match score engine (94% match breakdown by industry alignment, keyword relevance, and company size) with auto-generated cold outreach generator.
8. **08. Your Work, Connected**: 6 modular interconnected cards (*Calendar & Planning*, *Projects & Deliverables*, *Connections & Integrations*, *Earnings & Invoices*, *Notifications*, and *One Personalized Workspace*).
9. **09. How It Works**: 4-step sequential roadmap from onboarding setup to daily high-output execution.
10. **10. The CreatorOS / Levia AI Philosophy**: Core principles (*Context Over Generic AI*, *Connected Work Over Scattered Tools*, *Human Direction, AI Assistance*).
11. **11. Final CTA**: High-impact cosmic call to action with dual CTAs (`Get Started →` and `Explore Features →`).
12. **12. Footer**: Brand identity, mission, system status indicator, structured links, and legal disclosures.

---

## ⚡ Motion & Slide-Down Mechanics

- **Cinematic Slide-Down**: Smooth scrolling triggered by the navigation links, hero buttons, and floating indicator.
- **IntersectionObserver Reveals**: Staggered entry animations as sections scroll into view.
- **Interactive Persona Carousel**: The 4 dots switch between the 4 creator archetypes.
- **3D Mouse Parallax & Cursor Spotlight**: Real-time 60fps tracking on the glass spheres and cosmic lighting.
